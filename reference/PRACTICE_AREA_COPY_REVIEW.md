# Practice area copy review

The expanded practice-area text in `src/data/practiceAreaDetails.ts` is draft public marketing copy. An attorney should approve the English text and all translations before deployment. A qualified reviewer should also check the legal terminology and naturalness of each translation.

The car-accident text uses themes from the unchanged `Police Report Letter To New Potential Clients.docx`: conditional Massachusetts PIP coverage, medical costs and missed work, evidence preservation, and fact-dependent claims and deadlines. It intentionally omits the letter's numerical benefit limits, injury thresholds, and filing period. The letter does not support statements about the other five practice areas.

The motorcycle, truck, slip-and-fall, wrongful-death, and dog-bite details are new proposed copy based on the site's existing short descriptions and the requested educational topics. Review any statement about coverage, responsibility, available claims, procedures, or deadlines for accuracy and suitability for the firm's practice. The dialogs link to the site's existing legal disclaimer.
